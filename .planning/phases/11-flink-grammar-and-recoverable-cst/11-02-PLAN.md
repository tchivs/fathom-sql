<objective>
**As a** consumer, **I want to** parse Flink's everyday DML (INSERT/UPSERT, UPDATE, DELETE) and auxiliary statements (EXPLAIN, SHOW, DESCRIBE, ANALYZE, USE/SET/RESET) plus the Flink expression/type breadth (`=>` named arguments, Flink data types) into recoverable CST with localized diagnostics, **so that** the FLINK-02 everyday-statements requirement completes after the core-query tracer.

Purpose: Expand the Flink statement entry beyond SELECT to the full FLINK-02 everyday-statement set. Each production is a Flink-only path in parser/flink_grammar.mbt (never a Doris branch), reusing the shared recovery/span/lossless mechanisms. The bidirectional negative gates for this surface are frozen as fixtures (Flink-only DML rejected in Doris with FATHOM-PARSE-009; Doris-only DML/aux rejected in Flink). Every statement family ships with positive/negative/incomplete/recovery fixtures × strict/editor snapshots and lossless-replay assertions.
Output: parse_flink_insert / parse_flink_update / parse_flink_delete / parse_flink_explain / parse_flink_show / parse_flink_describe / parse_flink_analyze / parse_flink_use / parse_flink_set_reset / parse_flink_data_type, the extended dispatch arms, is_flink_insert_boundary, named-arg `=>` recognition, DML/aux flink-grammar snapshots, and the register rows.
</objective>
